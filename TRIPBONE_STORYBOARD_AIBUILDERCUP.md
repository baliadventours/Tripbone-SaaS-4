## 🛠️ Video Production & Recording Guidelines
1. **Screen Recording:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps. Set browser zoom to 110% for crisp typography.
2. **Narration:** Use a condenser microphone or clean AI vocal dubbing with clear diction.
3. **Background Music:** Upbeat, subtle tech/ambient corporate synth at 8–10% volume.
4. **Final Export:** MP4 format, H.264 video codec, under 300MB.
